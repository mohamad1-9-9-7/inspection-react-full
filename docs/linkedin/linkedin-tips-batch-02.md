## L-11 · Sick staff exclusion

Your best chef has diarrhoea. The kitchen is short-staffed. What do you do?

You send them home.

Food handlers with vomiting or diarrhoea must stay away from food until 48 hours after the symptoms stop.

Not "when they feel better". 48 hours after the last episode.

Why? Norovirus and other gut infections spread through tiny amounts on hands and surfaces. One sick handler can make an entire dining room ill.

What works in practice:
→ A short illness-reporting form at the start of every shift
→ A clear rule that reporting sickness is never punished
→ A manager who signs before the person returns to work

Short-staffed for 2 days is better than closed for 2 weeks.

## L-12 · Cleaning ≠ sanitizing

"We cleaned it." But did you sanitize it?

They are not the same thing:
🧽 Cleaning removes visible dirt, grease and food residue
🧪 Sanitizing reduces bacteria to a safe level

A sanitizer cannot work on a dirty surface. The dirt protects the bacteria.

The correct order:
1. Scrape / pre-rinse
2. Wash with detergent
3. Rinse
4. Sanitize at the right concentration
5. Leave for the contact time on the label
6. Air dry

Step 5 is the one most teams skip. A spray-and-wipe in 3 seconds rarely does the job.

## L-13 · Ice is food

Ice is food. Treat it like food.

The ice machine is one of the most neglected pieces of equipment in many kitchens and bars, and one of the most common places mould and slime show up during inspections.

Simple rules:
✅ Use a dedicated scoop and store it outside the ice
✅ Never scoop with a glass. If it chips, you will never find the pieces
✅ No bottles, cans or food stored in the ice bin
✅ Clean and sanitize the machine on the manufacturer's schedule, and record it
✅ Check the water filter

If you would not serve it on a plate, do not put it in a drink.

## L-14 · Signs of pests

Pests rarely show themselves. They leave clues.

What your team should report immediately:
🐭 Droppings along walls, under shelves and behind equipment
🐭 Gnaw marks on packaging, cables or doors
🪳 Egg cases or a musty smell in dark, warm areas
🪰 Flies around drains and waste areas
📦 Torn bags and spilled product in dry stores

Prevention beats treatment:
→ Seal gaps around pipes and doors
→ Keep doors closed and fly screens intact
→ No food left out overnight
→ Clean under and behind equipment, not just around it
→ A licensed pest-control contractor with a site map and visit reports

A pest sighting is a non-conformity, not a joke in the group chat.

## L-15 · Chemical storage

Detergent in a water bottle. Sanitizer in an unlabelled spray. Both on the shelf above the vegetables.

This is how chemical contamination happens.

The basics:
🧴 Chemicals stored in a separate, locked area, never above or next to food
🏷️ Original containers or properly labelled ones
📄 Safety Data Sheets (SDS) available to staff
🧪 Correct dilution, using a dispenser or measuring cup instead of guessing
🧤 PPE available and actually used
🚫 Never reuse a food container for chemicals, or a chemical container for food

The cleaning chemicals that protect your food can also harm it.

#FoodSafety #HSE #ChemicalSafety #FoodHygiene

## L-16 · Foreign bodies

A customer finds a piece of blue plastic in their meal. Where did it come from?

Foreign body complaints are some of the most damaging, and some of the most preventable.

Common sources:
💍 Jewellery (only a plain wedding band is generally accepted)
🩹 Plasters (they should be blue and detectable)
🖊️ Pen caps, hair clips, false nails
🔩 Loose screws and worn equipment parts
🪟 Broken glass and brittle plastic

What prevents it:
→ A glass & hard-plastic register, checked regularly
→ Hairnets and beard covers
→ No loose items in pockets above the waist
→ A clear procedure for breakages: stop, isolate, clean, record

Prevention is cheaper than a complaint.

## L-17 · Use By vs Best Before

"Use By" and "Best Before" do not mean the same thing.

📅 USE BY = Safety
After this date the food may be unsafe, even if it looks and smells fine. Do not use, sell or serve it.

📅 BEST BEFORE = Quality
After this date the food may lose flavour or texture, but it is usually still safe if stored correctly. (Always follow local regulations for selling it.)

Use By is usually found on high-risk chilled foods: fresh meat, fish, ready meals, dairy.
Best Before is usually on dry, canned and frozen foods.

Mixing these up either wastes good food or puts customers at risk.

## L-18 · Quarantine / hold area

Rejected. Returned. Suspect. Waiting for a decision.

Where does that product sit right now?

If the answer is "on the same shelf as everything else", it will end up being used or sold by mistake.

Every site needs a clearly marked HOLD area:
🔴 Physically separated, ideally caged or locked
🏷️ Every item tagged: reason, date, who put it there
📋 Recorded in a hold register
✅ Released or disposed of only by an authorized person
🗑️ Disposal documented, with evidence

"I thought someone else would deal with it" is not a food safety system.

#FoodSafety #QualityAssurance #Warehouse #ISO22000

## L-19 · New staff, first day

The highest-risk person in your kitchen today might be the one who started this morning.

New staff don't know your rules yet. Before they touch food, cover:
1. Handwashing: when and how
2. Illness reporting
3. Uniform, jewellery and hair rules
4. Where raw and ready-to-eat foods are kept
5. Temperature rules: chill, cook, hold
6. Allergens: what to say if a customer asks
7. Who to call when something goes wrong

Then sign it, record it, and refresh it.

Training that isn't recorded is training an auditor will assume never happened.

#FoodSafety #Training #Onboarding #HACCP #Hospitality

## L-20 · Food safety culture

You can have a perfect HACCP plan and still fail.

Because systems don't keep food safe. People do.

Signs of a strong food safety culture:
✅ Staff report problems without fear
✅ Managers follow the same rules they enforce
✅ Records are completed at the time, not before the audit
✅ Near misses are discussed, not hidden
✅ "Why?" is welcomed, not punished

Signs of a weak one:
❌ "We only do it when the inspector is coming"
❌ Records filled in with the same pen, same handwriting, same temperature
❌ Problems found by the customer first

Culture is what your team does when nobody is watching.
