Introduction

Paper records are no longer enough

In today's food market it is not enough to be safe — you have to prove it, on any day, to any inspector.

Paper files and scattered spreadsheets mean missing records, temperature readings nobody sees in time, and days of searching before every inspection. HACCP, ISO 22000 and regulators all call for one digital system that is traceable and audit-ready at any moment.

INSPECT PRO gives you one live platform your team fills on the phone and management follows the same day — so any record is in the inspector's hands in minutes, not days.

About us

Built by food-safety people,
for food-safety teams

INSPECT PRO is a UAE-based company that builds digital systems for food safety, quality and compliance.

The platform was born inside the daily work of a multi-branch meat company — real chillers, real receiving checks, real municipality inspections. Every screen was shaped by the people who fill the records and the QA teams who face the inspector; that is why it is fast on a phone at the branch and complete enough for management and auditors.

📍 Based in the UAE English & Arabic Cloud — no server

With INSPECT PRO, food businesses can:

Digitise daily records

Turn paper HACCP forms into phone checklists — in the same layout.

Trace products from supplier to branch

The item code links shipment, receiving, storage and branches in one search.

Close non-conformities with evidence

Every case has a reference number, an owner and documented proof before it closes.

Stay inspection-ready every day

Complete records and clear indicators for every branch, on any day.
